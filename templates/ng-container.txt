ng-container : Grouping elements

 - marks a location in the template without rendering a real element
 - rendering components dynamically with ngComponentOutlet
 - rendering templates dynamically with ngTemplateOutlet
 - apply structural directives eg, ngIf, ngFor
 - elements inside ng-container can inject directives or anything that the directive provides.
 eg. 
 <ng-container theme="dark">
  <profile-pic />
  <user-bio />
</ng-container>
